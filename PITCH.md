# LearnLens — 60-second pitch

Many students enter a new lesson carrying gaps from the previous one, but instructors often discover those gaps only after teaching has already begun.

LearnLens is a pre-class learning diagnostic system. After a lesson, students answer just three to five questions covering the prerequisite concepts for the next class.

The system analyzes their responses, identifies which concepts are causing difficulty, and places students into three simple learning states: Ready, Needs Reinforcement, or Needs Support.

Before the next class, the instructor sees a dashboard showing the class learning gaps and a focused recommendation about what needs reinforcement.

The goal is simple: move from teaching based on assumptions to teaching based on evidence of what students actually understand.

This MVP demonstrates that complete workflow using a Python Functions lesson.
